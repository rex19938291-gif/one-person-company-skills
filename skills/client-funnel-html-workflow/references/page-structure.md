# Three-page funnel checklist

## Homepage

1. Outcome-led hero and one primary CTA
2. Audience problem and reframing
3. Brand/method introduction
4. Free resource CTA
5. Three-step method or service map
6. Values or decision principles
7. Verified testimonials or clearly labeled scenarios
8. Closing CTA

## About page

1. Story-led hero
2. Origin and human context
3. Story one: problem, turn, lesson
4. Story two: exploration, proof, method
5. Story three: belief, promise, invitation
6. Relevant experience and trust boundaries
7. CTA

## Free-course page

1. Specific course promise
2. Non-submitting preview form
3. Three learning outcomes
4. Audience fit
5. Verified proof or scenario disclaimer
6. Instructor introduction
7. Repeated CTA
