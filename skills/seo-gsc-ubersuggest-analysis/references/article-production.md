## Intake

Collect these fields before drafting:

- `文章類型`: A information, B commercial investigation, or C transaction.
- `文章主題` and `目標讀者`.
- `核心關鍵字` and available `長尾關鍵字`.
- Main reader problem and intended action.
- Target site/publishing context and region.
- Sources or official facts that must be included.

For A, collect the concepts to explain: definition, use, benefits, method, process, mistakes, risks, and suitable audience. First-hand observations and a planned outline are useful.

For B, collect the products/services, item count, candidate list, ranking logic, comparison criteria, target audience, region, and current sources for price, specifications, reviews, opening hours, availability, warranty, and service area.

For C, collect the product/service name, verified features, advantages, use cases, audience, pain points, process, price/plans, proof, warranty, after-sales, contact method, and CTA.

## Safe defaults and blockers

When only secondary preferences are missing, use Taiwan Traditional Chinese; professional, clear, friendly, non-academic, non-pushy tone; second-person introductions; four to eight FAQs; and a short English lowercase hyphenated slug.

Ask before drafting if the topic, target reader, core keyword, product/service facts, or intended action is missing. Do not infer a commercial claim from a topic alone.

Never invent features, prices, results, reviews, testimonials, certifications, rankings, addresses, opening hours, service areas, company history, or guarantees. Use replaceable fields such as `[品牌名稱]`, `[官方價格]`, or `[聯絡方式]` when the user has not provided verified information.

## Common output

Return in this order:

```text
子目錄網址建議：英文小寫、連字號分隔、簡短
SEO標題：包含核心關鍵字與讀者利益
Meta Description：約100至150字，自然包含核心關鍵字、目標讀者與閱讀利益

文章主標題
從讀者情境、痛點、誤解、風險或市場現象切入的前言

主要段落一
段落小引言
細分標題與內容

主要段落二
段落小引言
細分標題與內容

後續主要段落
FAQ（通常4至8題）
品牌／產品／服務CTA
查證與待補資料（需要時）
```

Use visually clear bold headings, but do not print technical `H1`, `H2`, or `H3` labels in the article. Keep FAQ near the end and CTA immediately after FAQ.

The default reasoning rhythm is `痛點 → 原因／現況 → 具體做法 → 限制／風險 → 正確觀念 → 下一步`. Remove a section if it does not serve the search intent.

## A information

Use for knowledge explanations, teaching, process guides, introductions, and problem solving.

Suggested structure:

1. `[核心關鍵字]是什麼？先掌握基本概念`
 - Plain-language definition.
 - Difference from a commonly confused concept.
 - Suitable readers and use cases.
2. `[核心關鍵字]有哪些好處與用途？`
 - Three concrete benefits tied to reader problems.
 - Limitations and conditions.
3. `[核心關鍵字]怎麼做？完整流程`
 - Confirm goal and constraints.
 - Gather and verify information.
 - Compare or select methods.
 - Execute and record.
 - Review and adjust.
4. `執行[核心關鍵字]常見錯誤`
 - Explain each error's cause, impact, and correction.
5. FAQ, then an educational or consultation CTA supported by supplied brand facts.

## B commercial investigation

Use for recommendations, comparisons, rankings, fee guides, and selection advice.

Suggested structure:

1. `選擇[核心關鍵字]前要先注意什麼？`
 - Need, budget, scope, limitations, and use case.
2. `[核心關鍵字]推薦評選標準`
 - State criteria before naming items: price, features, suitable audience, convenience, support, region, warranty, and public official information.
3. One repeatable block per item:
 - Name and verified description.
 - Supported features.
 - Suitable audience.
 - Current price or `請洽官方`.
 - Caveats and source date when relevant.
4. Comparison table with only comparable, sourced fields.
5. `如何從推薦名單中做選擇？`
 - Price-focused, quality-focused, first-time, and special-need paths.
6. Local conditions when applicable, FAQ, and a non-deceptive own-brand CTA.

Do not call an item “best,” “number one,” or “most recommended” without a current, defensible methodology and source. Prefer `適合重視X的讀者` or `可列入評估`.

## C transaction

Use for product sales, service pages, brand conversion, booking, purchase, and lead generation.

Suggested structure:

1. Connect the offer to a verified reader pain point without exaggeration.
2. `為什麼你可能需要[核心關鍵字]？`
3. `[產品名稱]有哪些核心特色？`
 - Translate each verified feature into a user benefit and use case.
4. `[產品名稱]適合哪些人？`
 - State unsuitable conditions or a safer alternative when relevant.
5. `怎麼使用或申請？`
6. `價格與方案` using current official data or explicit placeholders.
7. Limitations, warranty, after-sales, and FAQ.
8. CTA with one clear next step: purchase, booking, consultation, quote, trial, or plan review.

Add testimonials or customer stories only when the user supplies genuine, authorized material. Never create outcomes, names, or metrics.

## Style and formatting

- Open with a reader situation, pain point, misconception, risk, or market change. Avoid `以下將介紹` and `本文將介紹`.
- Keep one main idea per paragraph and explain specialist terms when needed.
- Use short paragraphs suitable for mobile reading; use lists for steps and tables for comparisons.
- Do not add unnecessary spaces between Chinese, English, and numerals: use `3000字`, `SEO文章`, and `10家公司`.
- Preserve official brand spelling when the official name contains spaces.
- Keep person references consistent: second person for guides, first-person plural only for a brand's own service, third person for neutral comparisons.
- Default target length is about 3,000 Chinese characters or more unless the brief sets another length.
- For local SEO, include only verified city/district, service-area, transport, appointment, opening-hour, and local-market facts.
- Before handoff, complete the required `$humanizer-zh` pass. Keep functional steps, lists, and comparisons, but remove decorative three-part parallelism, repeated contrast formulas, generic AI openings/conclusions, and unnatural Taiwan Chinese without changing protected content.

## Evidence and risk

Verify laws, policies, taxes, subsidies, health, finance, prices, product specifications, store information, opening hours, statistics, rankings, certifications, current people or organizations, transport, and service areas.

Prefer government/regulator, official brand, original research/formal document, professional institution, edited media, then user-supplied internal material. State dates when freshness matters. Separate facts, estimates, assumptions, and user claims. Treat GSC data as actual performance and Ubersuggest volume/difficulty/traffic as external estimates.

Do not write unsupported claims such as `保證有效`, `絕對安全`, `業界第一`, `全台唯一`, `最多人推薦`, `零風險`, `一定能排名`, `一定能成交`, or `最低價`. Do not fabricate testimonials, sales numbers, market share, government endorsement, medical effects, customer results, or source citations. If evidence is missing, omit the claim or put it in `查證與待補資料`.

## Final checklist

### Intent and structure

- [ ] Title, introduction, section order, FAQ, and CTA match A/B/C intent.
- [ ] Slug is English, lowercase, hyphenated, short, and descriptive.
- [ ] SEO title and article title contain the core keyword naturally.
- [ ] Major headings use the core keyword only where grammatical and useful.
- [ ] Every major section has a short transition paragraph before subheadings.
- [ ] Meta description is approximately 100–150 Chinese characters and states reader benefit.

### Keywords and content

- [ ] Long-tail and related terms are distributed by topic, not stuffed.
- [ ] The article answers the reader's actual decision or problem.
- [ ] One paragraph handles one main idea; examples, lists, and tables improve comprehension.
- [ ] The draft meets the requested length; default is about 3,000 Chinese characters or more.
- [ ] No repeated ideas, empty slogans, or stiff formulaic openings.
- [ ] The required `$humanizer-zh` final pass is complete: no decorative three-part parallelism, repeated contrast formula, canned AI opening/conclusion, forced `首先／其次／最後` segmentation, or unnatural Taiwan Chinese. Functional sequences remain when they help the reader act.

### Evidence and conversion

- [ ] Prices, specifications, policies, statistics, rankings, local details, and current claims are verified.
- [ ] Estimates are labeled as estimates and sources/dates are clear.
- [ ] No fabricated claims, testimonials, certifications, guarantees, or customer results.
- [ ] Brand placement is relevant and supported by supplied facts.
- [ ] CTA states one clear next step and missing brand fields remain placeholders.
- [ ] `查證與待補資料` lists unresolved evidence, dates, client confirmations, and replacement fields.
- [ ] Facts, source labels, names, prices, dates, URLs, CTA wording, quotations, and commercial/legal terms still match the verified copy after humanization.
